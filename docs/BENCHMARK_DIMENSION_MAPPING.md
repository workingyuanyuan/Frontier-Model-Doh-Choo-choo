# Benchmark to Capability Mapping

> Mapping version: `benchmark-dimensions-v2`
> Last reviewed: 2026-10-03
> Purpose: capability semantics only. Source availability, freshness, weights and acquisition state belong elsewhere.

The six capability dimensions are Reasoning（推理）, Knowledge（知識）, Comprehension（理解）, Coding（程式）, Agentic（代理）and Language（語言）, defined in [FRONTIER_POLICY.md](FRONTIER_POLICY.md). The canonical IDs are `reasoning`, `knowledge`, `comprehension`, `coding`, `agentic` and `language`.

| Dimension             | Primary measured endpoint                                                                    |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reasoning（推理）     | Derive a solution through inference, calculation or proof construction.                      |
| Knowledge（知識）     | Recall facts or apply established specialist standards and domain rules.                     |
| Comprehension（理解） | Interpret supplied documents, workbooks, charts or transcripts and integrate their evidence. |
| Coding（程式）        | Produce, modify or repair executable software.                                               |
| Agentic（代理）       | Complete a task through tool selection, interaction and environment actions.                 |
| Language（語言）      | Produce language satisfying linguistic quality or instruction constraints.                   |

The approved assignments for the 54 active benchmarks are recorded below. The measured endpoint determines the primary category; the use of tools, code or professional material can be a secondary relationship.

The authoritative Phase 1 taxonomy is [benchmarks-v2.json](../data/mappings/benchmarks-v2.json); its approved quality rules are [benchmark-quality-v2.json](../data/mappings/benchmark-quality-v2.json). The frontier generator consumes these versioned inputs. Legacy mapping and display policy inputs provide compatibility until the Phase 2 scoring consumer migration.

Each Benchmark has exactly one primary capability dimension in v2. Secondary dimensions describe capabilities required by the task but do not receive duplicated score weight.

## Comprehension（理解）

GDP.xlsx (`gdp-xlsx`) maps primarily to Comprehension, with Reasoning, Agentic and Knowledge secondary. Its scored endpoint is a final answer derived from workbook evidence, calculations, cross-sheet relationships and business rules. Tools support inspection; the core ability is reconstructing the workbook logic. The 70 professional tasks span 12 domains. Sources: [official methodology](https://surgehq.ai/blog/gdp-xlsx) and [scoring convention](https://github.com/surge-ai/gdp-xlsx#grading), reviewed 2026-10-03.

GDP.pdf (`gdp-pdf`) uses Artificial Analysis' All-pass rate: the percentage of attempts satisfying every task criterion. Its primary dimension is Comprehension, with Reasoning, Agentic and Knowledge as secondary relationships. The AA implementation supplies prepared PDF text and page images to supported models and runs five attempts per task. Source: [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdp-pdf), reviewed 2026-10-03.

Chartography (`chartography`) maps primarily to Comprehension, with Reasoning and Knowledge secondary: it requires reading professional charts, interpolating values and combining visual evidence with domain conventions. The imported metric is the organizer's leaderboard Pass@1 percentage. Source: [Surge AI](https://surgehq.ai/benchmarks/chartography), reviewed 2026-10-02.

## Mapping rules for future additions

1. Read the Benchmark paper, methodology or official task description before mapping.
2. Map the measured endpoint, not every capability needed to obtain the answer.
3. Assign exactly one primary v2 capability dimension from the six above.
4. Record secondary relationships instead of duplicating full score weight.
5. Preserve version, task set, metric, grader and agent/harness distinctions.
6. Do not add a deprecated or superseded Benchmark merely because historical model scores are abundant.

EnterpriseBench: CoreCraft (`enterprisebench-corecraft`) maps primarily to Agentic, with Reasoning and Language secondary. Agents navigate an enterprise environment, use tools, apply company policies and complete customer support tasks. The imported main leaderboard percentage is task pass rate requiring all rubric criteria to pass. Source: [Surge AI methodology](https://surgehq.ai/blog/enterprisebench-corecraft), reviewed 2026-10-03.

Riemann-bench (`riemann-bench`) maps primarily to Reasoning, with Knowledge and Agentic secondary. Research-level mathematics requires deriving a verifiable closed-form answer; coding and search support that endpoint. Main leaderboard percentages are imported directly. The [official methodology](https://surgehq.ai/blog/riemann-bench-a-benchmark-for-moonshot-mathematics) records removal of the initial one-hour timeout; historical paper run counts are not assumed for current rows. Reviewed 2026-10-03.

DAYJOB: Healthcare (`dayjob-healthcare`) maps primarily to Agentic, with Reasoning and Knowledge secondary. It evaluates long-horizon work across clinical, operational, payer, pharmacy and compliance environments, including finding relevant records, judging next steps and completing deliverables. Scores are mean reward percentages over tasks, each averaging rewards from five attempts with errored trials excluded. Sources: [official leaderboard](https://surgehq.ai/benchmarks/dayjob-healthcare) and [scoring convention](https://github.com/surge-ai/dayjob#grading), reviewed 2026-10-03.

## Benchmark quality policy

The [quality policy](../data/mappings/benchmark-quality-v2.json) excludes `aime`, `programbench` and `proofbench` from scoring. ProofBench is semantically Reasoning; its six-model frontier scores are 99–100, with mean 99.5, population standard deviation 0.5 and two distinct values. The user approved its exclusion for insufficient discrimination on 2026-10-03. The [focused quality review](analysis/2026-10-03-proofbench-quality-review.json) preserves the cohort, exact scores, source evidence locators and [earlier review](refresh/2026-09-08-benchmark-saturation.json).

The limited benchmarks are `aa-lcr`, `gpqa-diamond`, `legal-bench`, `mmlu-pro` and `tax-eval-v2`. A selected dimension must contain at least two other, non-limited benchmarks per limited benchmark (`minOtherBenchmarksPerLimited: 2`). Apply this rule to the benchmark’s current primary dimension. Secondary relationships receive no duplicated score weight.
